Roadmap to the platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

One playthrough is enough, around 200 hours in total, and everything is single-player: no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Free roam carries on after the credits, so almost everything can be finished after the story — with one exception in Chapter 1. Keep cheats off your trophy run: they switch trophies and saving off.

The 100% completion checklist

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Everything you did before the credits already counts, and nothing on the checklist is missable. It asks for, in full: every robbery (all 8 bank vaults and all 39 gang operations), every underwater discovery, a gold medal in every Shooting Range challenge, all drone races, and all 15 of Bret’s Target Decks. The rest is a share: 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 100 of 255 animal studies, 15 of 18 meditations, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts and 100 WAiNK scans. The trophy “A Long Road” pops when the checklist hits 100%.

Roadmap and checklist figures: GTABase, “GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Achievements List” — see Sources & method.

Sources & method

Every trophy’s name, description and grade is shown as PlayStation publishes the list — the kit’s list agrees with Exophase and PSNProfiles on all 55 names, descriptions and grades. The guides are written in our own words for this page; they draw on the guides below and cite them beside each trophy, so a reader can check every specific location or step. Nothing is copied from another guide.

    How the flags were decided

    • Hidden: the 28 trophies PlayStation hides until earned — the story chapters and missions, the stranger quest finales, and the one-off moments with cryptic descriptions.
    • Missable: only “Positive Mental Attitude” is flagged missable. GTABase and PowerPyx both flag it as the one trophy that may be missable: a Chapter 1 meditation with Lucia disappears after that chapter, and the game has no mission replay. Every other trophy stays available in free roam after the story.
    • Kind: story, side quests, collectibles, activities, robbery, exploration, or one-off moments — from what the official description asks for.

    Your progress stays yours

    Ticking a trophy saves it in this browser only (localStorage). Export downloads a JSON file; import reads that file back. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and the page makes no network request at all.
